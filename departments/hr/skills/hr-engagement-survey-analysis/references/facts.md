# Human Resources: regulatory facts and figures

Dated, sourced figures the skills in this pack rely on. Rule for the assistant: use these values, cite the source line, and say "verify" for anything marked (verify) or not listed here. Figures marked (verify) were checked against secondary sources only.

## Employment law (Denmark)

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Written employment terms (ansættelsesbevisloven, implementing Directive (EU) 2019/1152) | Key terms within 7 calendar days of starting, the rest within 1 month | ansættelsesbevisloven |
| Holiday (ferieloven) | 2.08 days per month of employment, 25 days a year, concurrent holiday (samtidighedsferie); salaried staff get a holiday supplement (ferietillæg) of 1% | ferieloven |
| Parental leave (barselsloven, children born from 2 Aug 2022) | 24 weeks per parent after the birth, of which 11 weeks are earmarked; father or co-mother 2 weeks within the first 10 weeks, which count as part of their 24 weeks and their 11 earmarked weeks | barselsloven; borger.dk |
| Sickness | Employer pays the first 30 calendar days (employer period), then may claim reimbursement via NemRefusion if the employee is paid | sygedagpengeloven (verify) |
| Equal treatment | ligebehandlingsloven (gender), forskelsbehandlingsloven (age, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, political views, origin) | retsinformation.dk |
| Health questions in hiring (helbredsoplysningsloven) | Only about illness or symptoms of substantial significance for performing the specific work | helbredsoplysningsloven |
| Salaried employees (funktionærloven) | Notice and probation rules for funktionærer; restrictive covenants under ansættelsesklausulloven | retsinformation.dk |
| Workplace assessment (APV) | Written, reviewed at least every 3 years and when work changes; must cover the psychosocial working environment | arbejdsmiljøloven; Arbejdstilsynet |
| Whistleblower scheme | Required for employers with 50 or more employees | whistleblowerloven |
| Collective redundancies | Consultation and notification duties | masseafskedigelsesloven (Directive 98/59/EC) |
| Unsuccessful applications | Usual practice: delete within about 6 months unless the candidate consents to longer | Datatilsynet guidance |

## Pay transparency and equal pay

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| EU Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970 | Transposition deadline 7 Jun 2026; pay information before interview; ban on pay history questions | Directive Art. 5, 34 |
| Gender pay gap reporting under the directive | 250+ workers: first report by 7 Jun 2027, then yearly. 150 to 249: by 7 Jun 2027, then every 3 years. 100 to 149: by 7 Jun 2031, then every 3 years | Directive Art. 9 |
| Joint pay assessment trigger | Gap of 5% or more in a category of workers that is not justified by objective, gender-neutral criteria and not remedied within 6 months | Directive Art. 10 |
| Denmark transposition | Planned entry into force around 1 Jan 2027 per secondary sources; the draft act proposed different first-report timing for 150+; sources disagree, check the adopted act (verify) | WTW and GPA summaries (verify) |
| ligelønsloven pay statistics | Employers with at least 35 employees, where at least 10 men and 10 women are in the same DISCO job category | ligelønsloven |

## Training funding

VEU-godtgørelse mainly covers AMU and certain public courses; the competence funds (kompetencefonde) in collective agreements are often more relevant (verify eligibility).

## EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)

| Rule | Status and date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Prohibited practices (Art. 5), incl. inferring emotions in the workplace and education except for medical or safety reasons (Art. 5(1)(f)) | Apply since 2 Feb 2025 | 2024/1689 Art. 113 |
| New bans: AI generating non-consensual intimate images of identifiable people, and child sexual abuse material (Art. 5(1)(ba), (bb)) | Apply from 2 Dec 2026 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(7), 1(40) |
| AI literacy (Art. 4): providers and deployers must take measures to support a sufficient level of AI literacy (softened from "ensure") | Amended text applies since 27 Jul 2026 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(5) |
| Penalties (Chapter XII) | Apply since 2 Aug 2025; bans up to EUR 35m or 7% of turnover | 2024/1689 Art. 99 |
| Transparency (Art. 50): tell people they interact with an AI system unless obvious; deployers disclose deepfakes, and AI text published to inform the public on matters of public interest unless human-reviewed under editorial responsibility; providers mark synthetic output | Applies from 2 Aug 2026; generative systems already on the market must mark output by 2 Dec 2026 | 2024/1689 Art. 50; 2026/1744 Art. 1(39) |
| High-risk requirements (Chapter III Sections 1 to 3, incl. deployer duties in Art. 26) for Annex III systems (e.g. point 4: recruitment, selection, promotion, termination, task allocation, monitoring and evaluating workers) | Apply from 2 Dec 2027 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(40) |
| High-risk requirements for Annex I product systems | Apply from 2 Aug 2028 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(40) |
| Denmark: fines for breaching the Art. 5 bans | Lov nr. 467 af 14. maj 2025, in force 2 Aug 2025 | retsinformation.dk |

GDPR still applies in full alongside the AI Act: Art. 22 (no solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects), Art. 35 (DPIA), Art. 13/14 (transparency).
