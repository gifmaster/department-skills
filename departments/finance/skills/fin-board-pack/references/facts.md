# Finance: regulatory facts and figures

Dated, sourced figures the skills in this pack rely on. Rule for the assistant: use these values, cite the source line, and say "verify" for anything marked (verify) or not listed here. Figures marked (verify) were checked against secondary sources only.

## Bookkeeping and annual reports (Denmark)

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Retention | Bookkeeping material kept for 5 years | bogføringsloven |
| Digital bookkeeping systems | Phased duty to use a registered or compliant digital system | bogføringsloven; Erhvervsstyrelsen |
| Reporting classes | A, B, C (medium, large) and D (listed and state-owned) | årsregnskabsloven |
| Filing deadline | Classes B and C: 5 months after year end (31 May for a 31 Dec year end); class D: 4 months | årsregnskabsloven § 138 |
| Public annual reports | Available from the Central Business Register (CVR) | virk.dk |

## VAT (moms)

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Standard rate | 25% | momsloven |
| Filing frequency (by annual taxable turnover) | Half-yearly below DKK 5m; quarterly DKK 5m to 50m; monthly above DKK 50m (verify) | Skattestyrelsen |
| Deadlines | Monthly: 25th of the following month (exceptions apply); quarterly: 1st of the third month after the quarter (Q3 due 1 Dec); half-yearly: 1 Sep and 1 Mar | Skattestyrelsen |
| EU B2C distance sales | Above EUR 10,000 total EU B2C sales (current or previous year), the customer's country VAT applies; the One-Stop Shop (OSS) is an optional way to report it | Directive 2006/112/EC as amended |
| Reverse charge | B2B services from other EU businesses: buyer accounts for VAT | momsloven § 46 |
| Restaurant representation | 25% of the VAT is deductible when strictly business-related (verify) | Skattestyrelsen |
