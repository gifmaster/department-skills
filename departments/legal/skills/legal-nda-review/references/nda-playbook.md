# NDA playbook: positions, fallbacks and wording
Use: read when reviewing, negotiating or drafting an NDA or fortrolighedsaftale, to rate each clause green, amber or red and to pick redline wording (English and Danish).

Legal information and drafting support, not legal advice. Positions assume a Danish business under Danish law; adjust for the user's side (discloser, recipient or both). Mark any point you have not checked against Retsinformation or EUR-Lex as (verify) in the answer.

## How to use this playbook

1. Identify the user's side: mainly discloser, mainly recipient, or both (mutual).
2. For each clause below, compare the draft with the preferred position.
   - Green: matches preferred or acceptable fallback.
   - Amber: outside fallback but negotiable; propose the fallback wording.
   - Red: crosses the red line; delete or escalate before signing.
3. Anything that is not about confidentiality (IP assignment, non-compete, exclusivity, penalties) is a red flag by default, wherever it sits in the document.
4. Use the Danish wording when the counterparty is Danish or the NDA is in Danish.

## Quick rating sheet

| # | Clause | Preferred | Fallback | Red line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Definition | Marked or obviously confidential, both parties | Unmarked if reasonably understood as confidential | All information of any kind, one-way, when the user also discloses |
| 2 | Exclusions | Four standard exclusions plus legal disclosure | Same, with burden of proof on recipient | No exclusions |
| 3 | Use and purpose | Use only for a defined Purpose | Broader Purpose if still defined | Use "for any purpose" or undefined |
| 4 | Disclosees | Need-to-know staff, advisers, group companies, bound by duty | Named categories only, prior consent for others | Recipient liable without limit for anyone, or no advisers allowed |
| 5 | Term | Disclosure period 12 months; obligations 3 years after disclosure; trade secrets while secret | 5 years | Perpetual for all information |
| 6 | Return or deletion | On request, with backup and legal-hold carve-out | Certificate of deletion on request | Immediate physical return of all copies including backups, no carve-out |
| 7 | Remedies and penalties | Ordinary damages under Danish law; injunctive relief available | Capped, mutual, proportionate agreed damages | Fixed penalty per breach regardless of loss |
| 8 | Non-solicit and no-hire | None | Mutual, 6 to 12 months, active solicitation of staff directly involved, general adverts excluded | No-hire of any employee; non-compete of any kind |
| 9 | IP and licence | No transfer, no licence | Feedback licence limited to the feedback itself | Ideas, feedback or improvements vest in the other party |
| 10 | Warranties | Information "as is", no warranty | Warranty of right to disclose | Recipient warranties about results or use |

| 12 | Law and venue | Danish law, Copenhagen City Court or user's home court | Danish arbitration | Foreign law and courts between two Danish parties |

## 1. Definition of confidential information

- Preferred: information disclosed by either party for the Purpose that is marked confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.
- Fallback: all non-public information disclosed for the Purpose, marked or not, provided the NDA is mutual.
- Red line: one-way definition covering "all information" when the user will also show its own product, code or prices.
- Check: oral disclosures; whether the existence of the discussions is itself confidential; information disclosed before signature.

English wording:
> "Confidential Information" means all information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, which is marked as confidential or which a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.

Danish wording:
> "Fortrolige Oplysninger" betyder alle oplysninger, uanset form, som en Part ("Afgiveren") eller nogen på dennes vegne videregiver til den anden Part ("Modtageren") i forbindelse med Formålet, og som er mærket fortrolige, eller som en fornuftig person ud fra oplysningernes karakter eller omstændighederne ved videregivelsen ville forstå som fortrolige.

## 2. Exclusions

- Preferred: all four standard exclusions plus legally required disclosure.
- Fallback: same exclusions, recipient bears the burden of proof by written records.
- Red line: no exclusions at all.

English wording:
> The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information which the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in the Recipient's possession before disclosure, free of any duty of confidentiality; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Confidential Information. The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, a court or a competent authority, provided that, where lawful, it notifies the Discloser promptly and discloses no more than is required.

Danish wording:
> Forpligtelserne i denne Aftale gælder ikke for oplysninger, som Modtageren kan dokumentere (a) er eller bliver offentligt tilgængelige, uden at det skyldes brud på denne Aftale; (b) var i Modtagerens lovlige besiddelse før videregivelsen uden tavshedspligt; (c) lovligt er modtaget fra en tredjemand uden tavshedspligt; eller (d) er udviklet selvstændigt af Modtageren uden brug af de Fortrolige Oplysninger. Modtageren kan videregive Fortrolige Oplysninger, i det omfang det kræves efter lov, en domstol eller en kompetent myndighed, forudsat at Modtageren, hvor det er lovligt, straks underretter Afgiveren og ikke videregiver mere end påkrævet.

## 3. Permitted use and purpose

- Preferred: a specific Purpose, for example "evaluating a possible [service] agreement between the Parties".
- Fallback: a wider Purpose ("exploring a business relationship regarding [area]") if still bounded.
- Red line: no Purpose, or use for the recipient's own business generally.
- Watch for: reverse engineering, benchmarking or training AI models on the information. Add an express ban where code, data or models are shared.

English wording:
> The Recipient shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of [evaluating a possible agreement on (subject)] (the "Purpose") and shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any samples, software or models, or use Confidential Information to train or improve any machine learning model, save as necessary for the Purpose.

Danish wording:
> Modtageren må alene anvende de Fortrolige Oplysninger med henblik på [at vurdere en mulig aftale om (emne)] ("Formålet") og må ikke foretage reverse engineering, dekompilere eller analysere sammensætningen af prøver, software eller modeller eller anvende Fortrolige Oplysninger til at træne eller forbedre maskinlæringsmodeller, medmindre det er nødvendigt for Formålet.

## 4. Permitted disclosees

- Preferred: employees, officers, group companies, professional advisers and (where needed) financing parties who need to know for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as strict; recipient responsible for them.
- Fallback: list of categories; others with prior written consent (email sufficient).
- Red line: no disclosure even to legal advisers or auditors; or strict liability for any third party regardless of control.

English wording:
> The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its and its affiliates' employees, officers, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement. The Recipient is responsible for any breach by such persons as if it were its own.

## 5. Term and survival

- Preferred: disclosures covered for 12 months from signature; obligations last 3 years from each disclosure; trade secrets protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.
- Fallback: up to 5 years from disclosure (the skill's default range is 2 to 5 years).
- Red line: perpetual obligations for all information.
- Danish note: trade secrets are protected by statute irrespective of the contract term (lov om forretningshemmeligheder, lov nr. 309 af 25. april 2018 (verify number and date)).

English wording:
> This Agreement covers Confidential Information disclosed within [12] months from the date of signature. The obligations of confidentiality and non-use continue for [3] years from the date of each disclosure, save that obligations regarding trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

Danish wording:
> Denne Aftale omfatter Fortrolige Oplysninger, der videregives inden [12] måneder fra underskrift. Forpligtelserne til fortrolighed og begrænset anvendelse gælder i [3] år fra hver videregivelse, dog således at forpligtelser vedrørende forretningshemmeligheder gælder, så længe oplysningerne er forretningshemmeligheder efter gældende ret.

## 6. Return and destruction

- Preferred: on written request, return or delete; copies in automatic backups and copies required by law or internal compliance may be kept but stay confidential.
- Fallback: written confirmation of deletion within 30 days of request.
- Red line: duty to purge backups immediately, or to return information that has been combined into the recipient's own analyses with no carve-out.

English wording:
> On the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall within [30] days return or delete the Confidential Information and confirm this in writing, save that the Recipient may retain copies (a) held in automatic electronic backups until deleted in the ordinary course, and (b) required to be kept by law or regulation. Retained copies remain subject to this Agreement.

## 7. Remedies and penalty clauses

- Preferred: ordinary damages under Danish law; each party may seek an injunction (fogedforbud) without proving loss.
- Fallback: mutual, capped agreed damages that reflect a realistic estimate of loss, and set off against proven loss.
- Red line: fixed penalty per breach (konventionalbod) regardless of loss, especially one-way or out of proportion to the deal value.
- Danish law notes:
  - Agreed penalties are in principle binding between businesses, but a court may set aside or reduce a term that it would be unreasonable or contrary to good faith to rely on (aftaleloven § 36). Do not rely on § 36 as a defence; negotiate the clause out.
  - A penalty "per breach" can multiply quickly (each email can be argued to be a breach). Insist on a cap or deletion.

English wording (replacement):
> Each Party is liable for breach of this Agreement in accordance with the general rules of Danish law. Each Party acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy and that the other Party may seek injunctive relief.

Danish wording (replacement):
> Hver Part er ansvarlig for brud på denne Aftale efter dansk rets almindelige regler. Parterne anerkender, at erstatning ikke nødvendigvis er et tilstrækkeligt middel, og at den anden Part kan søge foreløbige retsmidler, herunder forbud.

## 8. Non-solicit and no-hire

- Preferred: none. An NDA protects information, not staff.
- Fallback: mutual, 6 to 12 months, limited to actively soliciting employees directly involved in the Purpose; general job adverts and unsolicited applications excluded.
- Red line: no-hire clause (ban on employing the other party's staff), or any non-compete or exclusivity.
- Danish law notes:
  - Agreements between businesses restricting an employee's ability to get a job at another business (jobklausuler) are banned under lov om ansættelsesklausuler (lov nr. 1565 af 15. december 2015) § 3, with an exception in § 4 for negotiations about a business transfer (max 6 months; employees with 3 months' service or less cannot be covered). Checked against Lovguiden's text of the act, not Retsinformation (verify).
  - Whether a pure "no active solicitation" clause (hvervningsforbud) falls within the jobklausul ban is not settled in the sources checked (verify). Keep any such clause narrow and mutual.
  - A non-compete between actual or potential competitors may also raise competition law issues (konkurrenceloven § 6 and TFEU Art. 101) (verify).

English fallback wording:
> For [12] months from the date of this Agreement, neither Party shall actively solicit any employee of the other Party who has been directly involved in the Purpose to leave that Party's employment. This does not restrict general recruitment advertising or hiring a person who responds to it or applies on their own initiative.

Danish fallback wording:
> I [12] måneder fra denne Aftales indgåelse må ingen af Parterne aktivt opfordre medarbejdere hos den anden Part, som har været direkte involveret i Formålet, til at fratræde deres stilling. Dette begrænser ikke almindelig rekruttering gennem stillingsopslag eller ansættelse af personer, der reagerer herpå eller søger på eget initiativ. [Verify against ansættelsesklausulloven §§ 3 and 4 before use.]

## 9. IP and licence

- Preferred: no transfer and no licence of IP; each party keeps its own pre-existing and independently developed technology.
- Fallback: discloser may use feedback it receives about its own products, but no rights in the recipient's technology.
- Red line: ideas, feedback, improvements or developments "during the evaluation" vest in the other party; any licence to the user's software.

English wording:
> All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement transfers or grants any licence or other right to any intellectual property right, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Each Party retains all rights in its pre-existing and independently developed technology and know-how.

Danish wording:
> Alle Fortrolige Oplysninger forbliver Afgiverens ejendom. Intet i denne Aftale indebærer overdragelse af eller licens til immaterielle rettigheder, ud over den begrænsede ret til at anvende Fortrolige Oplysninger til Formålet. Hver Part bevarer alle rettigheder til sin eksisterende og selvstændigt udviklede teknologi og knowhow.

## 10. Warranties

- Preferred: information provided "as is"; no warranty of accuracy or completeness; no obligation to disclose or to enter into any further agreement.
- Fallback: discloser warrants it has the right to disclose the information.
- Red line: recipient gives warranties about outcomes, security certifications or compliance not tied to its own handling.

English wording:
> Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party gives any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, and neither Party is obliged to disclose any information or to enter into any further agreement.

## 11. Liability

- Preferred: Danish law default (proven loss, causation, foreseeability); no cap for wilful or grossly negligent breach.
- Fallback: mutual cap on indirect loss; uncapped for wilful breach.
- Red line: one-way indemnity "for any and all losses", or liability for third parties outside the recipient's control.
- Note: limitations of liability are generally not upheld for gross negligence or intent under Danish law (verify).

## 12. Governing law and venue

- Preferred: Danish law, Copenhagen City Court (Københavns Byret) at first instance, or the user's home court.
- Fallback: arbitration under the rules of the Danish Institute of Arbitration (Voldgiftsinstituttet), seat Copenhagen, one arbitrator; or the counterparty's home law if it is an EU country and the NDA is low risk.
- Red line: foreign law and foreign courts between two Danish parties, or exclusive courts in a non-EU country.

English wording:
> This Agreement is governed by Danish law, excluding its choice of law rules. Any dispute shall be settled by the Copenhagen City Court (Københavns Byret) as the court of first instance.

Danish wording:
> Denne Aftale er underlagt dansk ret med undtagelse af danske lovvalgsregler. Enhver tvist afgøres ved Københavns Byret som første instans.

## 13. Assignment

- Preferred: no assignment without prior written consent, except to a group company or a buyer of the relevant business that takes over the obligations.
- Fallback: consent not to be unreasonably withheld.
- Red line: one-sided assignment rights.

English wording:
> Neither Party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or to an acquirer of all or substantially all of the relevant business that assumes the obligations in writing.

## Mutual or one-way

| Situation | Choose |
|---|---|
| Only the counterparty discloses (e.g. user receives a tender pack) | One-way in favour of the counterparty is acceptable; still apply exclusions and term |
| User will demo product, code, prices or roadmap | Mutual |
| User is the main discloser | One-way in favour of the user, or mutual |
| Nothing confidential will actually be shown | Consider no NDA; run the meeting on public material |

## Escalate to a lawyer when

- The NDA contains exclusivity, non-compete, IP assignment or penalties the counterparty refuses to remove.
- The information includes trade secrets central to the user's business, source code or personal data (consider a data processing or sharing agreement).
- Foreign law is insisted on, or the deal is an M&A or joint venture.

## Danish law notes (summary)

| Point | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Unreasonable terms, incl. penalties | Court may set aside or modify | aftaleloven § 36 |
| No-hire between businesses (jobklausuler) | Banned; exception during business transfer negotiations, max 6 months | lov om ansættelsesklausuler §§ 3, 4 |
| Trade secrets | Statutory protection independent of the NDA | lov om forretningshemmeligheder (verify number) |
| Non-compete between competitors | Possible competition law issue | konkurrenceloven § 6; TFEU Art. 101 (verify) |
