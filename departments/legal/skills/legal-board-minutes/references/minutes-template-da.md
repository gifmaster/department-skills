# Danish board minutes and written resolution templates (selskabsloven)
Use: read when drafting a Danish bestyrelsesreferat (protokol) or a skriftlig bestyrelsesbeslutning for an ApS or A/S, to record attendance, quorum, inhabilitet, decisions and signatures in line with selskabsloven.

Legal information and drafting support, not legal advice. Always check the company's articles of association (vedtægter) and rules of procedure (forretningsorden): they may set stricter quorum, majority or signing rules. Replace all [placeholders].

## Rules to apply

| Rule | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Quorum | The board is quorate when more than half of all members are represented, unless the articles require more. Decisions may not be taken unless, as far as possible, all members have had access to take part | selskabsloven § 124, stk. 1 |
| Alternates and proxies | An alternate steps in for an absent member; unless the board or articles decide otherwise, a member may in single cases give a proxy to another member if that is safe given the subject | selskabsloven § 124, stk. 2 |
| Majority | Simple majority unless the articles require a special majority; the articles may give the chair (or deputy chair) a casting vote | selskabsloven § 124, stk. 3 |
| Minutes | Where the board has several members, minutes (protokol) are kept and signed by all members present | selskabsloven § 128, stk. 1 |
| Dissent | A member present who disagrees with a decision may have their view entered in the minutes | selskabsloven § 128, stk. 2 |
| Rules of procedure | Boards with several members must adopt a forretningsorden; it may cover written and electronic meetings, minutes and deputies | selskabsloven § 130 |
| Conflicts of interest (inhabilitet) | A member may not take part in dealing with agreements between the company and the member, lawsuits against the member, or agreements or lawsuits with a third party where the member has a material interest that may conflict with the company's | selskabsloven § 131 |

Texts of §§ 124, 128, 130 and 131 checked on Lovguiden (LBK nr. 331 af 20/03/2025) and danskelove.dk, not on Retsinformation directly (verify).

Open points to check for each meeting:
- Whether a conflicted member counts towards the quorum for that item is not settled by § 131's wording; treat the item as needing a quorum of non-conflicted members unless the company's lawyer advises otherwise (verify).
- Written resolutions are not expressly regulated in selskabsloven; they are generally used where the articles or forretningsorden allow them and all members have had the opportunity to take part (§ 124, stk. 1). Check the forretningsorden (verify).
- Electronic signing (for example via a signing platform) is common; check the forretningsorden allows it.

## Checklist before signing

- [ ] Company name and CVR correct
- [ ] All board members listed: present, absent (afbud), attending by video, alternates or proxies
- [ ] Quorum recorded for the meeting and for any item where a member was conflicted
- [ ] Conflicts declared at the start and per item; conflicted member left the item (and the minutes say so)
- [ ] Each decision stated precisely (who, what, amount, conditions, authority to sign)
- [ ] Votes recorded where not unanimous; dissent entered on request (§ 128, stk. 2)
- [ ] Actions with owner and deadline
- [ ] Signed by all members present (§ 128, stk. 1); absent members acknowledge they have read the minutes if the forretningsorden requires it

### Phrases for common situations

| Situation | Danish wording |
|---|---|
| Not quorate | "Da kun [antal] af bestyrelsens [antal] medlemmer var til stede, var bestyrelsen ikke beslutningsdygtig, jf. selskabslovens § 124, stk. 1. Punktet blev drøftet, men beslutning udsættes til [næste møde / skriftlig beslutning]." |
| Not quorate on one item due to inhabilitet | "Efter [navn]s fratræden under punktet var der ikke et flertal af bestyrelsens medlemmer til stede til at behandle punktet. Beslutning udsættes til [ ]." (verify quorum rule) |
| Casting vote | "Ved stemmelighed var formandens stemme afgørende, jf. vedtægternes pkt. [x] og selskabslovens § 124, stk. 3." |
| Decision subject to condition | "Beslutningen er betinget af [ ]." |
| Confidential item | "Punktet blev behandlet fortroligt. Materialet opbevares hos [ ]." |

## 2. Skriftlig bestyrelsesbeslutning (written resolution template)

Use only where the articles or the forretningsorden allow written resolutions, or all members agree to the procedure (verify). Send to all members, give a clear deadline and record each member's response.

> **Skriftlig bestyrelsesbeslutning i [Selskabets navn] [ApS/A/S]**
> CVR-nr. [nr.]
>
> Undertegnede, som udgør samtlige medlemmer af bestyrelsen i [Selskabets navn] [ApS/A/S] ("Selskabet"), træffer herved i henhold til [forretningsordenens pkt. [x] / vedtægternes pkt. [x]] følgende beslutning uden afholdelse af møde:
>
> **1. Baggrund**
> [Kort beskrivelse af baggrunden og det materiale, der er sendt til bestyrelsen den [dato], fx udkast til aftale, bilag [x].]
>
> **2. Beslutning**
> Bestyrelsen beslutter at [præcis beslutning, fx "godkende, at Selskabet indgår [aftale] med [part], CVR [nr.], om [genstand] på de vilkår, der fremgår af udkast af [dato] (bilag 1)"].
>
> Bestyrelsen bemyndiger [navn/funktion] til at underskrive [aftalen] og alle dokumenter i forbindelse hermed på Selskabets vegne.
>
> **3. Interessekonflikter**
> [Ingen af bestyrelsens medlemmer har oplyst at være inhabile vedrørende beslutningen, jf. selskabslovens § 131.]
> [[Navn] har oplyst [forhold] og deltager ikke i beslutningen. Beslutningen træffes af de øvrige [antal] medlemmer.]
>
> **4. Erklæring**
> Hvert medlem bekræfter ved sin underskrift at have modtaget og haft adgang til at gennemgå materialet, og at det accepterer, at beslutningen træffes skriftligt.
>
> Beslutningen træder i kraft, når [samtlige / det nødvendige flertal af] medlemmer har underskrevet. Svarfrist: [dato].
>
> | Navn | For / Imod | Dato | Underskrift |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | [Navn], formand | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
> | [Navn] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
> | [Navn] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
>
> Beslutningen indføres i Selskabets protokol.

Notes:
- If any member objects to the written procedure or asks for a meeting, hold a meeting instead (verify against the forretningsorden).
- Do not use a written resolution to avoid discussing a conflict of interest; record the conflict and who decided.
- Decisions that belong to the general meeting (for example amendments to the articles) cannot be made by the board, in writing or otherwise.
