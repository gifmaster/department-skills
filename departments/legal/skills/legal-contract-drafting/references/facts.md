# Legal: regulatory facts and figures

Dated, sourced figures the skills in this pack rely on. Rule for the assistant: use these values, cite the source line, and say "verify" for anything marked (verify) or not listed here. Figures marked (verify) were checked against secondary sources only.

## Data protection (GDPR and databeskyttelsesloven)

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Data subject requests (Art. 12(3)) | Answer within 1 month of receipt; extendable by 2 further months only if the data subject is told of the extension and the reasons within the first month | GDPR Art. 12(3) |
| Danish limits on access rights | Exceptions in databeskyttelsesloven § 22 (e.g. overriding private or public interests) | databeskyttelsesloven § 22 |
| Breach notification | Controller to Datatilsynet within 72 hours of becoming aware (Art. 33(1)); processor to controller without undue delay (Art. 33(2)); individuals without undue delay if high risk (Art. 34) | GDPR Art. 33, 34 |
| Processor contracts | Art. 28(3) content required. Commission standard clauses: Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/915. Datatilsynet has adopted Art. 28(8) standard clauses (EDPB Opinion 14/2019) | GDPR Art. 28 |
| Records of processing | Separate records for controllers (Art. 30(1)) and processors (Art. 30(2)) | GDPR Art. 30 |
| International transfers | Chapter V; EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy (importer must be certified); otherwise SCCs (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) plus a transfer impact assessment | GDPR Art. 44-49 |

## Consumer and marketing law

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Withdrawal right | 14 days for most distance and off-premises contracts; refund within 14 days | Directive 2011/83/EU; forbrugeraftaleloven |
| Withdrawal function (button) | From 19 Jun 2026 traders concluding distance contracts through an online interface must provide a withdrawal function | Directive (EU) 2023/2673 |
| Complaint about defects (reklamation) | 2 years for consumers; defects appearing within 1 year are presumed to have existed at delivery; complain within a reasonable time (within 2 months is always timely) | købeloven |
| Green claims | From 27 Sep 2026: ban on generic environmental claims without recognised excellent environmental performance, and on claims of neutral, reduced or positive impact based on greenhouse gas offsetting | Directive (EU) 2024/825 |
| Price reductions | Announced reductions must use the lowest price in the 30 days before the reduction as the reference | Directive 98/6/EC Art. 6a (inserted by 2019/2161) |
| Fake reviews | Banned; traders publishing reviews must state whether and how they ensure reviews come from real consumers | Directive (EU) 2019/2161 |
| Unsolicited electronic marketing | Prior consent needed for email, SMS and social media private messages to any recipient, businesses and named employees included; narrow existing-customer exception | markedsføringsloven § 10; Forbrugerombudsmanden spam guidance (2021) |
| Consumer ADR | Nævnenes Hus; the EU ODR platform closed on 20 Jul 2025, remove old ODR links | Regulation (EU) 2024/3228 (verify) |

## Collection and late payment

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Before collection steps | A written payment demand (påkravsskrivelse) with at least 10 days to pay | inkassoloven § 10 (check scope for business debtors) |
| Reminder fees | Max DKK 100 per reminder, max 3, at least 10 days apart, only if agreed or permitted | renteloven § 9 b, stk. 2 |
| B2B late payment | Statutory interest = Nationalbanken reference rate + 8 percentage points (§ 5); fixed compensation for recovery costs (currently DKK 310 per claim, amount set by executive order) | renteloven § 5, § 9 a, stk. 3 and 4 (verify current amount) |
| B2B payment terms | Max 30 days unless the creditor expressly agrees and it is not grossly unfair; 30 days for public authorities | renteloven § 3a, § 3b |

## EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)

| Rule | Status and date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Prohibited practices (Art. 5), incl. inferring emotions in the workplace and education except for medical or safety reasons (Art. 5(1)(f)) | Apply since 2 Feb 2025 | 2024/1689 Art. 113 |
| New bans: AI generating non-consensual intimate images of identifiable people, and child sexual abuse material (Art. 5(1)(ba), (bb)) | Apply from 2 Dec 2026 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(7), 1(40) |
| AI literacy (Art. 4): providers and deployers must take measures to support a sufficient level of AI literacy (softened from "ensure") | Amended text applies since 27 Jul 2026 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(5) |
| Penalties (Chapter XII) | Apply since 2 Aug 2025; bans up to EUR 35m or 7% of turnover | 2024/1689 Art. 99 |
| Transparency (Art. 50): tell people they interact with an AI system unless obvious; deployers disclose deepfakes, and AI text published to inform the public on matters of public interest unless human-reviewed under editorial responsibility; providers mark synthetic output | Applies from 2 Aug 2026; generative systems already on the market must mark output by 2 Dec 2026 | 2024/1689 Art. 50; 2026/1744 Art. 1(39) |
| High-risk requirements (Chapter III Sections 1 to 3, incl. deployer duties in Art. 26) for Annex III systems (e.g. point 4: recruitment, selection, promotion, termination, task allocation, monitoring and evaluating workers) | Apply from 2 Dec 2027 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(40) |
| High-risk requirements for Annex I product systems | Apply from 2 Aug 2028 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(40) |
| Denmark: fines for breaching the Art. 5 bans | Lov nr. 467 af 14. maj 2025, in force 2 Aug 2025 | retsinformation.dk |

GDPR still applies in full alongside the AI Act: Art. 22 (no solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects), Art. 35 (DPIA), Art. 13/14 (transparency).
