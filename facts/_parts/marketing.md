## Marketing law (Denmark and EU)

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| General | Claims must be truthful, documented and not misleading | Unfair Commercial Practices Directive; markedsføringsloven |
| Green claims | From 27 Sep 2026: ban on generic environmental claims without recognised excellent environmental performance, and on neutral, reduced or positive impact claims based on offsetting | Directive (EU) 2024/825 |
| Price reductions | Reference price = lowest price in the 30 days before the reduction | Directive 98/6/EC Art. 6a; Danish prismærkningsbekendtgørelse (verify citation) |
| Advertising identification | Paid content and influencer posts must be clearly marked, in Denmark "Reklame" or "Annonce" at the start | markedsføringsloven § 6; Forbrugerombudsmanden influencer guidance |
| Email and SMS marketing | Prior consent for any recipient, businesses included; § 10(2) existing-customer exception (address from a sale, own similar products, free easy opt-out at collection and in every message); a consent request by email is itself marketing | markedsføringsloven § 10 |
| Cookies | Cookiebekendtgørelsen is supervised by Erhvervsstyrelsen; consent must meet the GDPR standard; Datatilsynet supervises personal data collected via cookies | Erhvervsstyrelsen; Datatilsynet |
| Fake reviews | Banned; state whether and how reviews are verified | Directive (EU) 2019/2161 |
| Listed companies | Inside information goes out as a company announcement first (MAR Art. 17); no embargoed pre-briefing of inside information (MAR Art. 10) | Regulation (EU) 596/2014 |
