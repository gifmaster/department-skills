## EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)

| Rule | Status and date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Prohibited practices (Art. 5), incl. inferring emotions in the workplace and education except for medical or safety reasons (Art. 5(1)(f)) | Apply since 2 Feb 2025 | 2024/1689 Art. 113 |
| New bans: AI generating non-consensual intimate images of identifiable people, and child sexual abuse material (Art. 5(1)(ba), (bb)) | Apply from 2 Dec 2026 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(7), 1(40) |
| AI literacy (Art. 4): providers and deployers must take measures to support a sufficient level of AI literacy (softened from "ensure") | Amended text applies since 27 Jul 2026 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(5) |
| Penalties (Chapter XII) | Apply since 2 Aug 2025; bans up to EUR 35m or 7% of turnover | 2024/1689 Art. 99 |
| Transparency (Art. 50): tell people they interact with an AI system unless obvious; deployers disclose deepfakes, and AI text published to inform the public on matters of public interest unless human-reviewed under editorial responsibility; providers mark synthetic output | Applies from 2 Aug 2026; generative systems already on the market must mark output by 2 Dec 2026 | 2024/1689 Art. 50; 2026/1744 Art. 1(39) |
| High-risk requirements (Chapter III Sections 1 to 3, incl. deployer duties in Art. 26) for Annex III systems (e.g. point 4: recruitment, selection, promotion, termination, task allocation, monitoring and evaluating workers) | Apply from 2 Dec 2027 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(40) |
| High-risk requirements for Annex I product systems | Apply from 2 Aug 2028 | 2026/1744 Art. 1(40) |
| Denmark: fines for breaching the Art. 5 bans | Lov nr. 467 af 14. maj 2025, in force 2 Aug 2025 | retsinformation.dk |

GDPR still applies in full alongside the AI Act: Art. 22 (no solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects), Art. 35 (DPIA), Art. 13/14 (transparency).
