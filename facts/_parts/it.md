## Cybersecurity

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NIS2 (Directive (EU) 2022/2555) | Risk management measures Art. 21(2)(a) to (j); management body approval, oversight and training (Art. 20) | NIS2 |
| NIS2 incident reporting (Art. 23) | Early warning within 24 hours of awareness; notification within 72 hours; final report no later than 1 month after the notification | NIS2 Art. 23 |
| Denmark NIS2 law | Lov nr. 434 af 6. maj 2025, in force 1 Jul 2025; sector authorities supervise; significant incidents are reported through the joint reporting solution on virk.dk; check registration duty | retsinformation.dk; virk.dk |
| Size thresholds (general rule) | Medium or larger: 50+ staff, or annual turnover AND balance sheet both above EUR 10m. Large: 250+ staff, or turnover above EUR 50m AND balance sheet above EUR 43m; linked and partner enterprises count; some entities in scope regardless of size | NIS2 Art. 2; Recommendation 2003/361/EC |
| GDPR security | Art. 32 appropriate technical and organisational measures; breaches to Datatilsynet within 72 hours (Art. 33) | GDPR |
| DORA | Applies to financial entities and critical ICT third-party providers from 17 Jan 2025 | Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 |
| Backup baseline | 3-2-1-1-0: 3 copies, 2 media, 1 off-site, 1 offline or immutable, 0 restore errors (tested) | Industry practice |
| Data Act cloud switching | Customers' right to switch data processing services; switching charges phased out by 12 Jan 2027 | Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 |
