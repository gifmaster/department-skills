## Consumer rights

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Withdrawal right | 14 days for most distance and off-premises sales; refund within 14 days of withdrawal | Directive 2011/83/EU; forbrugeraftaleloven |
| Withdrawal function | Online distance contracts need a withdrawal function from 19 Jun 2026 | Directive (EU) 2023/2673 |
| Defect complaints (Denmark) | 2-year complaint period; defects within 1 year presumed present at delivery; complain within a reasonable time (2 months always timely); remedies: repair, replacement, price reduction, cancellation | købeloven |
| Norway | 5-year complaint period for goods meant to last substantially longer than 2 years (verify) | forbrukerkjøpsloven |
| Cancelling subscriptions | Making cancellation harder than sign-up can be an unfair commercial practice; Forbrugerombudsmanden expects it to be as easy as signing up. The DSA dark-pattern ban (Art. 25) covers online platforms only. A general EU rule is proposed in the Digital Fairness Act | UCPD; Forbrugerombudsmanden; Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 Art. 25 |
| Reviews | Fake reviews banned; traders publishing reviews must state whether and how they ensure reviews come from real customers | Directive (EU) 2019/2161 |
| Dispute resolution | Nævnenes Hus (Denmark); ARN (Sweden); Forbrukerrådet (Norway); the EU ODR platform closed 20 Jul 2025 | (verify) |

## Accessibility

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) | Applies from 28 Jun 2025 to specific products and services (e-commerce, consumer banking, electronic communications, e-books, some passenger transport); support services of those must give accessibility information; microenterprises providing services exempt; conformity usually shown against EN 301 549 (WCAG 2.1 AA) | Directive 2019/882; Danish tilgængelighedslov (verify) |

## Incidents and product safety

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Personal data breach | Controller notifies Datatilsynet within 72 hours (GDPR Art. 33(1)); processor notifies the controller without undue delay (Art. 33(2)); high-risk breaches to individuals (Art. 34) | GDPR |
| Product recalls | General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988, recall notice requirements in Art. 36 | GPSR |
| NIS2 | Check reporting duties if the company is in scope | NIS2 Art. 23 |

## Staff monitoring

Quality scoring with AI is high-risk under AI Act Annex III point 4 (from 2 Dec 2027); GDPR Art. 22 and transparency apply now; Danish collective agreements generally require 6 weeks' notice of new control measures (verify agreement).
