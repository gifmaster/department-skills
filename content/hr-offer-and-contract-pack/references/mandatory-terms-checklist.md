# Mandatory employment terms checklist and Danish offer letter
Use: Read when checking a contract or ansættelsesbevis for required information, stating deadlines for written terms, or drafting a Danish offer letter.

Source: Lov nr. 501 af 16. maj 2023 om ansættelsesbeviser og visse arbejdsvilkår (ansættelsesbevisloven), in force 1 Jul 2023, implementing Directive (EU) 2019/1152. Item numbers below are § 3, stk. 2, nr. 1 to 15, and the deadline split is § 3, stk. 1 (checked against the act on retsinformation.dk). Other figures follow facts/hr.md (last verified 2026-11-16); anything else is marked (verify).

## 1. Deadlines at a glance

| What | Deadline | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Items nr. 1 to 5, 7 and 10 to 12 | No later than 7 calendar days after employment has started | § 3, stk. 1 |
| Items nr. 6, 8, 9 and 13 to 15 | No later than 1 month after employment has started | § 3, stk. 1 |
| Form | In writing, on paper or electronically if the employee can store and print it and the employer keeps proof of sending or receipt | § 3, stk. 3 |
| Reference instead of stating the term | Allowed by referring to laws, regulations or collective agreements, except for nr. 1 to 6, 12 and 14, which must be stated directly | § 3, stk. 4 |
| Work abroad for more than 4 consecutive weeks | Information before departure | § 4 |
| Changes to terms | In writing as soon as possible and no later than the day the change takes effect | § 5 |
| Probation | Cannot exceed 6 months; in fixed-term employment it must be proportionate to the duration (verify exact wording) | § 6 |
| Breach | Compensation (godtgørelse) under § 13, up to 13 weeks' pay, 20 weeks if aggravating (verify amounts and conditions) | § 13 |

Practical rule: give the full contract before day 1. The 7-day and 1-month deadlines are latest dates, not targets.

### Items outside the act to check in the same pass

| Tick | Item | Rule or reason | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Restrictive covenants (konkurrence-, kunde- or kombinationsklausul) | Regulated by ansættelsesklausulloven: generally only for employees in a particularly trusted position, time-limited and with compensation (verify current limits, often cited as max 12 months and 40% or 60% compensation). Always legal review | ansættelsesklausulloven (verify) |
| [ ] | Intellectual property and content created in the job | Clear assignment wording; legal review for creative or development roles | Contract practice |
| [ ] | Confidentiality | Standard clause; preferred over a non-compete for most roles | Contract practice |
| [ ] | Sickness and pay | Employer pays the first 30 calendar days; reimbursement via NemRefusion after that if the employee is paid (verify) | sygedagpengeloven (verify) |
| [ ] | Pay transparency | No pay history questions; pay information before interview; Danish act planned around 1 Jan 2027 (verify) | Directive (EU) 2023/970 Art. 5 |
| [ ] | Personal data | Privacy notice to the employee (GDPR Art. 13) | GDPR |
| [ ] | Consistency | Salary, hours, start date and title identical in offer letter and contract | Quality check |

### Positions on common clauses

| Clause | Preferred | Fallback | Red line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probation (funktionær) | 3 months with 14 days' notice | Shorter probation | Over 3 months with shortened notice for a funktionær (verify); over 6 months for anyone (§ 6) |
| Non-compete | None; use confidentiality | Narrow customer clause after legal review | Any clause without legal review, compensation or a trusted-position basis |
| Notice | "Efter funktionærloven" | Longer employer notice by agreement | Employer notice shorter than statutory |
| Collective agreement | Name it, or "Ingen overenskomst" | | Silence on nr. 14 |

## 3. Danish offer letter template (tilbudsbrev)

Send by email with the contract attached or to follow. Keep figures identical to the contract.

**Emne:** Tilbud om ansættelse som [stilling] hos [virksomhed]

Kære [fornavn]

Tak for nogle gode samtaler. Vi er glade for at kunne tilbyde dig stillingen som [stilling] hos [virksomhed]. Her er de vigtigste vilkår:

| Vilkår | |
|---|---|
| Stilling | [stilling], med reference til [navn, titel] |
| Tiltrædelse | [dato] |
| Arbejdssted | [adresse]{, med mulighed for [antal] hjemmearbejdsdage om ugen efter aftale} |
| Arbejdstid | [37] timer om ugen{, med fleksibel tilrettelæggelse inden for [tidsrum]} |
| Løn | [beløb] kr. om måneden, udbetalt bagud den sidste bankdag i måneden |
| Pension | [virksomhed] indbetaler [x] % og du [y] % af lønnen til [pensionsselskab] fra [første ansættelsesdag / efter x måneder] |
| Personalegoder | [fx mobiltelefon, internet, frokostordning med egenbetaling på [x] kr. om måneden] |
| Ferie | Efter ferieloven, med løn under ferie og ferietillæg |
| Prøvetid | [3] måneder |
| Opsigelse | Efter funktionærloven |
| Overenskomst | [navn på overenskomst / Ingen overenskomst] |
| Ansættelsesvilkår i øvrigt | Fremgår af ansættelseskontrakten |

Tilbuddet gælder til og med [dato]. Du accepterer ved at svare "Ja tak" på denne mail. Derefter sender vi din ansættelseskontrakt, som du underskriver digitalt, senest [dato].

{Tilbuddet er betinget af [fx tilfredsstillende referencer / gyldig arbejds- og opholdstilladelse].}

Har du spørgsmål, er du meget velkommen til at ringe til mig på [telefon].

Vi glæder os til at byde dig velkommen.

Venlig hilsen
[Navn]
[Titel], [virksomhed]

### Notes for the drafter

- Conditions in the offer must be job-related; do not make an offer conditional on health information beyond what helbredsoplysningsloven allows.
- A verbal yes plus this letter can already form a binding agreement; agree internally before sending.
- If a collective agreement applies, check its pay, hours and pension terms before sending.
- English version on request: translate the table one to one and keep the Danish legal terms in brackets (for example "notice per the Salaried Employees Act (funktionærloven)").
