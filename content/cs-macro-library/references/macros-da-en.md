# Webshop macro starter library (Danish and English)
Use: Read when building or reviewing saved replies for a webshop; adapt these 15 macros to the shop's policies and helpdesk placeholder syntax instead of writing from scratch.

Legal points follow facts/customer-service.md (last verified 2026-11-16) and forbrugeraftaleloven chapter 4 (checked on lovguiden.dk). Anything else is marked (verify).

## How to use

- Placeholders: `[first_name]`, `[order_no]`, `[agent_name]`, `[shop_name]`, `[date]`, `[link]` and others in square brackets. Map them once to the helpdesk's own syntax (field codes differ between systems) and keep the same names across languages.
- Danish uses du-form. Sign-off: "Venlig hilsen / [agent_name], [shop_name]"; English: "Kind regards / [agent_name], [shop_name]". Shown once here, omitted below.
- Every macro is a starting point: add one sentence that shows you read the customer's message.
- Statutory rights always win. A voluntary returns policy (for example "unopened only") never limits the 14-day withdrawal right or the 2-year complaint right. Every return macro has an escape route for faulty goods.
- Never promise refunds, compensation, dates or exceptions that the shop has not approved.
- Review cycle: check all macros every 6 months and whenever a policy or law changes; owner: [team lead].

## 2. Macro texts and usage notes

### M01 Where is my order

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Tak for din besked. Din ordre [order_no] blev sendt [date] med [carrier]. Du kan følge pakken her: [tracking_link]. Den forventes at være fremme [expected_date]. Er den ikke kommet til den tid, så skriv igen, så undersøger vi det med det samme.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Thanks for your message. Your order [order_no] was dispatched on [date] with [carrier]. You can track it here: [tracking_link]. It is expected to arrive on [expected_date]. If it has not arrived by then, just write again and we will look into it straight away.

**When not to use:** the parcel is past the expected date or tracking has not moved for several days (use M13); tracking says delivered but the customer has nothing (investigate, do not send a macro).

### M02 Return within 14 days (withdrawal)

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Selvfølgelig kan du fortryde dit køb. Du har 14 dages fortrydelsesret fra den dag, du modtog varen. Sådan gør du: 1) Giv os besked via [withdrawal_function_link] eller ved at svare på denne mail inden [deadline]. 2) Send varen retur senest 14 dage efter, at du har givet besked, til [return_address]{, med denne returlabel: [label_link]}. Returfragten betaler [du selv / vi]. Vi refunderer købsbeløbet inklusive den oprindelige standardfragt senest 14 dage efter, at vi har modtaget din besked, men vi kan vente, til vi har fået varen retur eller dokumentation for, at den er sendt. Du må gerne åbne og prøve varen, som du ville gøre i en butik. Er varen brugt mere end det, kan vi trække et beløb fra for værdiforringelse. Er der noget galt med varen, så skriv det, for så er det en reklamation, og den koster dig ikke noget.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Of course you can cancel your purchase. You have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the day you received the item. How to do it: 1) Tell us via [withdrawal_function_link] or by replying to this email before [deadline]. 2) Send the item back within 14 days of telling us, to [return_address]{, using this return label: [label_link]}. Return shipping is paid by [you / us]. We refund the price, including the original standard delivery charge, within 14 days of receiving your notice, but we may wait until we have the item back or proof that you have sent it. You may open and try the item as you would in a shop. If it has been used beyond that, we may deduct an amount for the loss in value. If something is wrong with the item, please tell us, because then it is a complaint and it will cost you nothing.

**Rules behind it:** forbrugeraftaleloven § 19 (14 days from physical possession; up to 12 months longer if the customer was not informed), § 22 (refund within 14 days including standard delivery, may withhold until goods or proof received), § 24 (customer returns within 14 days, pays direct return costs if informed, liable only for diminished value from handling beyond what is needed). Online contracts need a withdrawal function from 19 Jun 2026 (Directive (EU) 2023/2673).

**When not to use:** the item is faulty (M03); the product is excluded from withdrawal, for example custom-made or sealed hygiene goods unsealed (check forbrugeraftaleloven § 18, stk. 2, and escalate); the customer bought in a physical shop (no statutory withdrawal right, only the shop's policy).

### M03 Defect complaint (reklamation)

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Hvor ærgerligt, at [product] ikke virker, som den skal. Det vil vi gerne løse. Vil du sende et par billeder eller en kort video af fejlen og dit ordrenummer, hvis det ikke står i mailen? Så vender vi tilbage inden [response_time] med, hvordan vi ordner det, typisk ved reparation eller en ny vare. Det koster dig ikke noget, heller ikke fragten.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. I am sorry that [product] is not working as it should. We would like to put it right. Could you send a few photos or a short video of the fault, and your order number if it is not in your email? We will get back to you within [response_time] with how we will fix it, usually by repair or a replacement. It will cost you nothing, including shipping.

**Rules behind it:** købeloven: 2-year complaint period; defects appearing within 1 year are presumed present at delivery; complaint within a reasonable time, 2 months from discovery always timely; remedies repair or replacement first, then price reduction or cancellation; remedy free for the customer. Sections often cited: § 77 a, stk. 3 (presumption), §§ 78 to 78 c (remedies), § 81 (notice), § 83 (2 years) (secondary source, verify).

**When not to use:** clear signs of misuse or the complaint is more than 2 years after delivery (escalate, do not reject by macro); the customer demands a refund straight away (use cs-refund-returns-decision).

### M04 Refund status

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Tak fordi du spørger. Vi modtog din retur [received_date] og har sendt refunderingen på [amount] kr. [refund_date] til [payment_method]. Afhængigt af din bank kan der gå [x] bankdage, før beløbet står på kontoen. Kan du ikke se det efter [date], så skriv endelig igen.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Thanks for checking. We received your return on [received_date] and issued a refund of DKK [amount] on [refund_date] to [payment_method]. Depending on your bank it can take [x] banking days to show in your account. If you cannot see it after [date], please write again.

**When not to use:** the refund has not yet been issued and more than 14 days have passed since the withdrawal notice and receipt of the goods or proof of return (escalate and refund now); the amount differs from what the customer expects (explain the deduction individually).

### M05 Wrong item

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Undskyld, der er sket en fejl hos os. Du skulle have haft [ordered_item], men fik [received_item]. Vi sender den rigtige vare i dag, og du får et trackinglink på mail. {Du behøver ikke sende den forkerte vare retur. / Brug venligst denne gratis returlabel til den forkerte vare: [label_link].}

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Sorry, we made a mistake. You should have received [ordered_item] but got [received_item]. We are sending the right item today and you will get a tracking link by email. {You do not need to return the wrong item. / Please use this free return label for the wrong item: [label_link].}

**When not to use:** the right item is out of stock (combine with M12 and offer a refund); the customer no longer wants the item (treat as cancellation with full refund including shipping).

### M06 Damaged in transport

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Det er vi kede af. Vil du sende et billede af varen og af emballagen, gerne med fragtlabelen synlig? Så sender vi [en ny vare / en løsning] hurtigst muligt. Det koster dig ikke noget. Du skal ikke selv tage sagen med fragtfirmaet, det gør vi.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. We are sorry about this. Could you send a photo of the item and the packaging, ideally with the shipping label visible? We will then send [a replacement / a solution] as quickly as possible. It will cost you nothing. You do not need to deal with the carrier yourself; we will do that.

**Rules behind it:** in consumer sales the shop carries the risk until the customer receives the goods (købeloven, verify section). Do not make a carrier claim the customer's problem.

**When not to use:** the parcel was collected from a parcel shop days after notification and damage is disputed (escalate); high-value items where the carrier requires a damage report within a set time (follow the carrier process).

### M07 Address change

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Tak for besked. {Din ordre [order_no] er endnu ikke sendt, så vi har ændret adressen til: [new_address]. / Din ordre [order_no] er desværre allerede sendt. Vi har bedt [carrier] om at ændre adressen, men vi kan ikke love, at det lykkes. Du kan også selv prøve via [tracking_link].}

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Thanks for letting us know. {Your order [order_no] has not yet been dispatched, so we have changed the address to: [new_address]. / Your order [order_no] has unfortunately already been dispatched. We have asked [carrier] to change the address, but we cannot guarantee it. You can also try yourself via [tracking_link].}

**When not to use:** the request comes from a different email than the order and the new address is in another country or the order is high value (verify identity first: possible fraud).

### M08 Cancel subscription

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Vi har nu opsagt dit abonnement [subscription_name]. Din sidste levering er [last_delivery_date], og du bliver ikke trukket flere penge efter [last_charge_date]. Du får en bekræftelse på mail. Du kan altid starte igen på [link]. {Vil du hellere holde en pause, kan du sætte abonnementet på pause i op til [x] uger.}

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. We have now cancelled your subscription [subscription_name]. Your last delivery is on [last_delivery_date] and you will not be charged after [last_charge_date]. You will receive a confirmation by email. You can restart at any time at [link]. {If you would rather take a break, you can pause the subscription for up to [x] weeks.}

**Rules behind it:** cancelling should be as easy as signing up; making it harder can be an unfair commercial practice (UCPD; Forbrugerombudsmanden). Offer a pause once, at most, and only after confirming the cancellation.

**When not to use:** never use a retention script that delays or blocks the cancellation; if the subscription has a binding period, explain the end date individually.

### M09 Discount code not working

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Ærgerligt, at koden driller. Koden [code] gælder [conditions, fx "til og med [date] på ikke-nedsatte varer ved køb over [x] kr."]. {Jeg har tjekket, og din ordre opfylder betingelserne, så jeg har refunderet [amount] kr. til dig. / Din kurv indeholder [reason], og derfor virker koden ikke.} Skriv endelig, hvis du har spørgsmål.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Sorry the code is causing trouble. The code [code] is valid [conditions, for example "until [date] on full-price items for orders over DKK [x]"]. {I have checked and your order meets the conditions, so I have refunded DKK [amount] to you. / Your basket contains [reason], which is why the code does not apply.} Let us know if you have any questions.

**When not to use:** the code is from a third-party site the shop did not authorise (explain individually); several customers report the same failure (technical incident, escalate).

### M10 Invoice copy

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Du finder din faktura for ordre [order_no] vedhæftet{, med [company_name] og CVR [cvr] som du bad om}. Du kan også altid hente dine fakturaer under "Mine ordrer" på [link].

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Please find attached the invoice for order [order_no]{, made out to [company_name] with CVR [cvr] as requested}. You can also download your invoices at any time under "My orders" at [link].

**When not to use:** the customer asks to change the buyer or VAT details on an issued invoice (finance must issue a credit note and new invoice).

### M11 GDPR data request handoff

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Tak for din henvendelse om dine personoplysninger. Vi har sendt din anmodning videre til [dpo_or_privacy_team], som behandler den. Du hører fra dem senest [date, inden for 1 måned]. For at beskytte dine oplysninger kan de bede dig bekræfte, at det er dig, fx ved at svare fra den e-mailadresse, du handler med.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Thank you for your request about your personal data. We have passed it to [dpo_or_privacy_team], who will handle it. You will hear from them by [date, within 1 month]. To protect your data, they may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by replying from the email address you shop with.

**Rules behind it:** answer data subject requests without undue delay and within one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests with notice to the person (GDPR Art. 12(3)). Log the date received.

**When not to use:** never answer the request itself from support or send personal data by macro; if the message reports a data breach, escalate immediately (72-hour notification clock, GDPR Art. 33).

### M12 Out of stock

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Desværre er [product] udsolgt lige nu. Vi forventer at have den igen [restock_date / vi kender endnu ikke datoen]. Du kan {vente på din ordre / få pengene retur med det samme / vælge [alternative]}. Skriv, hvad du foretrækker, så ordner vi det.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Unfortunately [product] is out of stock right now. We expect it back [restock_date / we do not yet have a date]. You can {wait for your order / get a full refund straight away / choose [alternative]}. Let us know what you prefer and we will sort it out.

**When not to use:** the customer has already paid and the delay is open-ended (offer a refund as the default, do not hold the money without consent).

### M13 Delivery delay apology

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Undskyld, at din ordre [order_no] er forsinket. Jeg kan se, at [status, fx "pakken har stået stille hos fragtfirmaet siden [date]"]. Jeg har [action, fx "oprettet en efterlysning"], og jeg vender tilbage senest [date], også selvom jeg ikke har nyt. {Hvis du ikke længere ønsker varen, kan du få pengene retur.}

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. I am sorry that your order [order_no] is delayed. I can see that [status, for example "the parcel has not moved at the carrier since [date]"]. I have [action, for example "opened a trace"], and I will get back to you by [date], even if I have no news. {If you no longer want the item, you can have a refund.}

**When not to use:** a wider carrier or system outage affecting many orders (use cs-outage-communication).

### M14 Price match request

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Tak for tippet. {Vi matcher prisen fra [competitor_type] på varer, der er på lager og identiske, og din ordre lever op til det, så vi har refunderet [amount] kr. / Vi tilbyder desværre ikke prismatch, men [alternative, fx "du kan fortryde inden for 14 dage, hvis du hellere vil købe et andet sted"].}

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Thanks for letting us know. {We match prices from [competitor_type] on identical in-stock items, and your order qualifies, so we have refunded DKK [amount]. / Unfortunately we do not offer price matching, but [alternative, for example "you can withdraw within 14 days if you would rather buy elsewhere"].}

**When not to use:** the shop has no approved price match policy and the agent is tempted to improvise a discount (escalate to [team lead]). Price matching is voluntary.

### M15 Warranty versus complaint explanation

**DA:** Hej [first_name]. Godt spørgsmål. Du har altid 2 års reklamationsret efter købeloven, hvis varen har en fejl, der var der fra starten. Fejl, der viser sig inden for det første år, regnes som udgangspunkt for at have været der fra starten. Reklamationsretten gælder over for os som sælger. En garanti er noget ekstra, som [producenten / vi] giver frivilligt, og den kommer oveni din reklamationsret, ikke i stedet for. I dit tilfælde [explanation, fx "er varen købt for 14 måneder siden, så du kan både reklamere hos os og bruge producentens garanti"]. Send os gerne billeder af fejlen, så hjælper vi dig videre.

**EN:** Hello [first_name]. Good question. You always have a 2-year right to complain under Danish sales law (købeloven) if the item has a fault that was there from the start. Faults that appear within the first year are presumed to have been there from the start. That right is against us as the seller. A warranty (guarantee) is something extra that [the manufacturer / we] give voluntarily, and it comes on top of your complaint right, not instead of it. In your case [explanation, for example "the item was bought 14 months ago, so you can both complain to us and use the manufacturer's warranty"]. Please send us photos of the fault and we will help you further.

**When not to use:** the customer is in Norway (the complaint period can be 5 years for durable goods under forbrukerkjøpsloven, verify) or another country; wear and tear or damage the customer caused (escalate for an individual answer).

## 3. Usage notes for the team

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Read the whole message before choosing a macro | Mixed cases (late and damaged) need two macros merged |
| Faulty item mentioned anywhere: use M03 logic, not a return policy macro | Statutory complaint rights cannot be reduced by policy |
| Do not ask for card numbers, passwords or full CPR numbers | Personal data and fraud risk |
| Disputes about rights go to [team lead]; the customer can complain to Nævnenes Hus (verify) | Support does not give legal conclusions |
| Personalise the first line every time | Macros must sound human |
| Log macro ID in the ticket | Lets you measure use and review weak macros |
