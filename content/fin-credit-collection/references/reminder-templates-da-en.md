# Payment reminder templates (Danish and English)
Use: Read before drafting any rykker, final notice (påkrav) or reply to a disputed invoice; pick the B2B or consumer variant and fill the fee and interest lines from the rules in section 1.

Legal points follow facts/finance.md (last verified 2026-11-16). Amounts marked (verify) must be confirmed before sending. Disputed invoices never get a reminder: use section 6.

## 1. Fee, interest and timing rules

| Item | B2B (business customer) | Consumer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reminder fee | Max DKK 100 per reminder | Max DKK 100 per reminder | renteloven § 9 b, stk. 2 |
| Number of fee-bearing reminders | Max 3 for the same claim | Max 3 for the same claim | renteloven § 9 b, stk. 2 |
| Spacing | Fee only for reminders sent at least 10 days apart | Same | renteloven § 9 b, stk. 2 |
| Reason | Fee only for reminders sent for a reasonable cause (after the due date) | Same | renteloven § 9 b, stk. 1 |
| Departing by agreement | Higher fees only within § 9 a; get legal confirmation first (verify) | Cannot be changed to the consumer's detriment | renteloven § 9 b, stk. 4 |
| Fixed compensation | DKK 310 per claim (amount set by executive order). The act does not say whether it can be combined with reminder fees; check before charging both (verify) | Not applicable | renteloven § 9 a, stk. 3 and 4 |
| Statutory interest | Nationalbanken reference rate + 8 percentage points, from the due date | Same rate under § 5 (verify); from the due date if agreed in advance, otherwise from 30 days after the demand was sent (renteloven § 3, verify) | renteloven § 5 |
| Contractual interest | Only if the customer accepted the terms; otherwise use statutory | Only if validly agreed and not unreasonable (verify) | Contract |
| Before collection | Written payment demand (påkrav) giving at least 10 days to pay before further collection steps | Same | inkassoloven § 10 (verify) |
| Payment terms | Max 30 days B2B unless expressly agreed and not grossly unfair | | renteloven § 3a |

Interest formula: amount x annual rate / 100 x days overdue / 365. Show the calculation in the draft notes, not in the letter, unless the user wants it.

Suggested rhythm (adjust to policy):

| Step | When | Fee | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reminder 1 | 3 to 7 days after due date | Optional: DKK 0 (goodwill) or up to DKK 100 | Friendly, assume oversight |
| Reminder 2 | At least 10 days after reminder 1 | Up to DKK 100 | Firm, state consequences |
| Reminder 3 (final, påkrav) | At least 10 days after reminder 2 | Up to DKK 100 | Final, 10-day deadline, collection warning |
| Phone call | Before or with reminder 2 for large or key accounts | | |
| Collection | Only after the user decides, after the påkrav deadline | | |

Public-sector customers: check first that the invoice was sent as an e-invoice with the right EAN number and order reference; a missing e-invoice is a common reason for non-payment.

Do not threaten registration in a debtor register, legal action or collection unless the user has decided to take that step. Never threaten on a disputed claim.

## 5. Reminder 3: final notice with påkrav and collection warning

Send only if at least 10 days have passed since reminder 2, the claim is undisputed and the user has decided that collection is the next step. Give at least 10 days to pay (inkassoloven § 10, verify).

## 6. Reply to a disputed invoice

Written for B2B ("I/jer"); for consumers switch to du-form. Stop all reminders, fees and interest claims on the disputed part until resolved. Ask for specifics, offer to split undisputed and disputed amounts, and set a date. If the dispute turns into a formal breach or legal claim, hand off to legal-demand-letter.

## 7. Checks before sending

- [ ] Claim undisputed and invoice actually delivered (e-invoice for public sector)
- [ ] Fee no more than DKK 100, no more than 3 fee-bearing reminders, at least 10 days since the last one
- [ ] DKK 310 compensation only for B2B, amount and combination with fees confirmed (verify)
- [ ] Interest rate is the agreed rate the customer accepted, or reference rate + 8 with the current reference rate filled in
- [ ] Final notice gives at least 10 days and the user has approved the collection step
- [ ] Bank details, amounts and dates match the ledger
