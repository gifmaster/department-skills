# Data subject request response templates (Danish and English)
Use: read when writing to a data subject about an access or other rights request: acknowledgement, identity check, extension notice, full Article 15 response or partial refusal under databeskyttelsesloven § 22.

Legal information and drafting support, not legal advice. Use du-form for customers, employees and former employees unless the requester writes formally or via a lawyer (then use the lawyer's name and a formal tone). Replace all [placeholders].

## Deadlines and rules to apply

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Response deadline | 1 month from receipt by the organisation (any channel) | GDPR Art. 12(3) |
| Extension | Up to 2 further months for complex or numerous requests, only if the data subject is told of the extension and the reasons within the first month | GDPR Art. 12(3) |
| No action taken | Tell the data subject within 1 month why, and that they can complain to Datatilsynet and seek a judicial remedy | GDPR Art. 12(4) |
| Identity | If there are reasonable doubts, the controller may ask for information needed to confirm identity | GDPR Art. 12(6) |
| Fee | Free; a reasonable fee only for further copies or manifestly unfounded or excessive requests | GDPR Art. 12(5), 15(3) |
| Copy must not adversely affect others | Redact third party data | GDPR Art. 15(4) |
| Danish exceptions | Data subject's interest must yield to decisive private interests (stk. 1) or listed public interests (stk. 2, incl. nr. 9 protection of others' rights and nr. 10 enforcement of civil law claims) | databeskyttelsesloven § 22 (text checked on danskelove.dk, verify on Retsinformation) |

Timing notes:
- Ask for identity or clarification at once. Do not use an identity request to delay; check current EDPB guidance (Guidelines 01/2022 on the right of access) on whether the clock pauses (verify).
- "Copy" means a faithful and intelligible reproduction of the personal data; extracts or whole documents may be needed where context is necessary to understand the data (CJEU C-487/21).
- If the deadline has passed: send a holding reply today with an apology and a firm date, then the full response. The extension is no longer available. Document the cause. A late reply can lead to a complaint; there is no duty to self-report it to Datatilsynet.

## 2. Identity request

Ask only for what is proportionate. Prefer checks against data you already hold (for example replying from the registered email address, or logging in to the customer account). Do not ask for a copy of a passport or CPR number unless genuinely necessary.

### Danish

> Emne: Bekræftelse af din identitet, ref. [ref.]
>
> Kære [navn]
>
> Vi har modtaget din anmodning af [dato]. For at sikre, at vi ikke udleverer personoplysninger til den forkerte person, skal vi bede dig bekræfte din identitet.
>
> Du kan gøre det på en af følgende måder:
> - [svare på denne e-mail fra den e-mailadresse, du har registreret hos os]
> - [logge ind på din konto og sende anmodningen via [sted]]
> - [oplyse [kundenummer / medarbejdernummer] og [en oplysning, vi allerede har, fx postnummer]]
>
> [Hvis anmodningen kommer fra en anden på dine vegne: Vi skal også bede om en underskrevet fuldmagt fra dig.]
>
> Vi behandler din anmodning, så snart vi har modtaget bekræftelsen. Oplysningerne bruges kun til at bekræfte din identitet og slettes derefter.
>
> Venlig hilsen
> [Navn]
> [Virksomhed]

## 4. Full response to an access request (Art. 15)

Checklist before sending:
- [ ] Copy of the personal data (Art. 15(3)), organised by source, in a commonly used electronic form if requested electronically.
- [ ] Third party data redacted, with a redaction log kept internally.
- [ ] All items (a) to (h) of Art. 15(1) answered, plus Art. 15(2) if data goes outside the EU/EEA.
- [ ] Any exceptions applied are explained (see section 5).
- [ ] Secure delivery (encrypted email, secure portal or Digital Post).

### Danish

> Emne: Svar på din anmodning om indsigt, ref. [ref.]
>
> Kære [navn]
>
> Vi henviser til din anmodning om indsigt af [dato]. Vi kan bekræfte, at vi behandler personoplysninger om dig. Nedenfor får du de oplysninger, du har ret til efter databeskyttelsesforordningens artikel 15.
>
> **1. Formål med behandlingen (art. 15, stk. 1, litra a)**
> [Fx administration af dit ansættelsesforhold, lønudbetaling, opfyldelse af kundeaftale, markedsføring.]
>
> **2. Kategorier af personoplysninger (litra b)**
> [Fx navn, kontaktoplysninger, CPR-nummer, løn- og kontooplysninger, fravær, e-mails og beskeder, præstationsdata.]
>
> **3. Modtagere (litra c)**
> [Fx lønbureau, pensionsselskab, Skattestyrelsen, it-leverandører som databehandlere.] [Hvis modtagere uden for EU/EØS: angiv land og overførselsgrundlag (art. 15, stk. 2), fx EU-Kommissionens standardkontraktbestemmelser.]
>
> **4. Opbevaringsperiode (litra d)**
> [Fx "Oplysninger om løn opbevares i 5 år efter regnskabsårets udløb af hensyn til bogføringsloven. Øvrige personaleoplysninger slettes [x] år efter fratrædelse."]

> **5. Dine rettigheder (litra e)**

>

>
> [Hvis undtagelser er anvendt, indsæt afsnit fra skabelon 5.]
>
> Har du spørgsmål til svaret, er du velkommen til at kontakte [navn/funktion] på [e-mail/telefon].
>
> Venlig hilsen
> [Navn]
> [Virksomhed]

## 5. Partial refusal (databeskyttelsesloven § 22)

Before using: assess document by document; record the reasons internally; disclose everything not covered. The exception is applied narrowly. Typical grounds for private employers: stk. 1 (decisive private interests, including the company's own, for example internal deliberations about a pending legal claim), stk. 2, nr. 9 (protection of others' rights and freedoms) and nr. 10 (enforcement of civil law claims). Have a lawyer confirm before relying on it in a dispute. Legal privilege may also justify withholding lawyer advice (verify).

>
