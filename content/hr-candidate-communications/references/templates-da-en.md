# Candidate message templates (Danish and English)
Use: Read before drafting any invitation, rejection, feedback, reference request or retention consent message to a candidate; adapt the matching template rather than writing from scratch.

All templates use du-form in Danish and a warm, plain tone. Fill every [placeholder]; delete optional lines in {curly brackets} if they do not apply. Legal points follow facts/hr.md (last verified 2026-11-16).

## 0. Rules that apply to every message

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Protected grounds | Never mention or hint at gender, pregnancy, marriage, family plans, age, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, political views or origin (ligebehandlingsloven; forskelsbehandlingsloven). If the user's notes contain such a reason, stop: warn the user, do not use it, and recommend they document the job-related grounds and talk to their employer organisation or a lawyer. |
| Health | Do not ask about health. Only illness or symptoms of substantial significance for the specific work may be asked about (helbredsoplysningsloven). Asking whether the candidate needs practical adjustments for the interview is fine. |
| Written reasons | Anything written to a candidate can become evidence. Keep reasons short, true and job-related. |
| Pay | Under the EU Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970 Art. 5, candidates must get pay information before the interview and must not be asked about pay history. Danish transposition is planned around 1 Jan 2027 (verify). Use the optional pay line in template 1. |
| Retention | Unsuccessful applications are usually deleted within about 6 months unless the candidate consents to longer (Datatilsynet guidance). Every rejection states this and gives a contact for data questions. |
| Subject line | Always include role title and company name. |

## 1. Interview invitation

### Dansk

**Emne:** Invitation til samtale: [stilling] hos [virksomhed]

Kære [fornavn]

Tak for din ansøgning til stillingen som [stilling]. Vi vil meget gerne møde dig til en samtale.

- **Dato og tid:** [ugedag] den [dato] kl. [tid] (ca. [varighed] minutter)
- **Sted:** [adresse / link til videomøde]
- **Du møder:** [navn, titel] og [navn, titel]
- **Forberedelse:** [fx "Vi beder dig forberede et kort oplæg på 10 minutter om ..." / "Du behøver ikke forberede noget særligt."]
{- **Løn:** Lønnen for stillingen er [lønspænd eller startløn] [pr. måned ekskl. pension / inkl. pension].}

Passer tidspunktet ikke, så svar blot med to eller tre tidspunkter, der passer dig bedre. Har du brug for praktiske hensyn til samtalen, fx adgangsforhold, så skriv endelig.

Bekræft venligst, at du kommer, senest [dato].

Vi glæder os til at tale med dig.

Venlig hilsen
[Navn]
[Titel], [virksomhed]
[Telefon]

### English

**Subject:** Interview invitation: [role] at [company]

Dear [first name]

Thank you for applying for the role of [role]. We would very much like to meet you for an interview.

- **Date and time:** [weekday] [date] at [time] (about [duration] minutes)
- **Place:** [address / video link]
- **You will meet:** [name, title] and [name, title]
- **Preparation:** [for example "Please prepare a 10-minute presentation on ..." / "You do not need to prepare anything in particular."]
{- **Pay:** The pay for this role is [range or starting salary] [per month excluding / including pension].}

If the time does not suit you, just reply with two or three alternatives. If you need any practical adjustments for the interview, such as step-free access, please let us know.

Please confirm by [date].

We look forward to speaking with you.

Kind regards
[Name]
[Title], [company]
[Phone]

## 2. Rejection after screening (no interview)

### Dansk

**Emne:** Din ansøgning: [stilling] hos [virksomhed]

Kære [fornavn]

Tak for din ansøgning til stillingen som [stilling] og for den interesse, du har vist [virksomhed].

Vi har fået mange kvalificerede ansøgninger, og vi har valgt at gå videre med kandidater, hvis erfaring passer tættere på det, stillingen kræver lige nu. Derfor bliver du desværre ikke indkaldt til samtale denne gang.

Vi gemmer din ansøgning i [6] måneder og sletter den derefter. Hvis du vil have den slettet før, eller har du spørgsmål om, hvordan vi behandler dine oplysninger, kan du skrive til [e-mail til persondata].

Vi håber, at du vil holde øje med andre stillinger hos os.

Venlig hilsen
[Navn]
[Titel], [virksomhed]

### English

**Subject:** Your application: [role] at [company]

Dear [first name]

Thank you for applying for the role of [role] and for your interest in [company].

We received many strong applications and have decided to continue with candidates whose experience is a closer match for what the role needs right now. We will therefore not be inviting you to an interview this time.

We will keep your application for [6] months and then delete it. If you would like it deleted sooner, or have questions about how we handle your data, please write to [data protection email].

We hope you will keep an eye on future vacancies with us.

Kind regards
[Name]
[Title], [company]

## 4. Feedback message (on request)

Rules: two or three points, all tied to the job requirements or the case; at least one genuine strength; one concrete development point with an example; no comparison with named candidates; no protected grounds. Check with the hiring manager that each point is documented in the interview notes.

### Dansk

**Emne:** Feedback på din samtale: [stilling] hos [virksomhed]

Kære [fornavn]

Tak fordi du bad om feedback. Her er de vigtigste punkter fra vores samtaler:

- **Det, du gjorde rigtig godt:** [kompetence fra stillingsopslaget], fx [konkret eksempel fra samtalen].
- **Det, vi gerne ville have set mere af:** [kompetence fra stillingsopslaget]. I [casen / samtalen] savnede vi [konkret, observerbar ting, fx "en plan for, hvordan du følger op på budgetafvigelser undervejs"].
- {**Et tredje punkt:** [jobrelateret iagttagelse].}

Beslutningen var tæt, og vi håber, at feedbacken kan bruges fremover. Du er velkommen til at ringe til mig på [telefon], hvis du vil uddybe noget.

Venlig hilsen
[Navn]
[Titel], [virksomhed]

### English

**Subject:** Feedback on your interview: [role] at [company]

Dear [first name]

Thank you for asking for feedback. Here are the main points from our interviews:

- **What you did very well:** [competence from the job ad], for example [specific example from the interview].
- **What we would have liked to see more of:** [competence from the job ad]. In [the case / the interview] we missed [specific, observable point, for example "a plan for following up on budget deviations during the project"].
- {**A third point:** [job-related observation].}

It was a close decision and we hope the feedback is useful. You are welcome to call me on [phone] if you would like to discuss anything.

Kind regards
[Name]
[Title], [company]

## 5. Reference request (to a referee named by the candidate)

Rules: only contact referees the candidate has named and agreed to; ask job-related questions; do not ask about health, sick leave, family or union membership; tell the referee what the information is used for.

### Dansk

**Emne:** Referenceforespørgsel: [kandidatens fulde navn]

Kære [referencens navn]

[Kandidatens navn] har søgt stillingen som [stilling] hos [virksomhed] og har oplyst dig som reference med sit samtykke.

Vil du have 15 minutter til en kort samtale i løbet af de næste dage? Jeg vil gerne høre om:

- hvilken rolle [fornavn] havde hos jer, og i hvilken periode
- [fornavns] styrker i arbejdet med [nøgleopgave fra stillingen]
- områder, hvor [fornavn] kunne udvikle sig
- om du ville ansætte [fornavn] igen i en lignende rolle

Oplysningerne bruges kun i denne rekruttering og slettes sammen med ansøgningen. Foreslå gerne et tidspunkt, eller ring på [telefon].

Venlig hilsen
[Navn]
[Titel], [virksomhed]

### English

**Subject:** Reference request: [candidate full name]

Dear [referee name]

[Candidate name] has applied for the role of [role] at [company] and has named you as a referee with their consent.

Could you spare 15 minutes for a short call in the next few days? I would like to hear about:

- [first name]'s role with you and the period
- [first name]'s strengths in [key task from the role]
- areas where [first name] could develop
- whether you would hire [first name] again in a similar role

The information is used only for this recruitment and is deleted together with the application. Please suggest a time, or call me on [phone].

Kind regards
[Name]
[Title], [company]

## 6. Retention and consent wording (keeping an application longer than about six months)

Rules: consent must be separate, specific, informed and freely given; silence is not consent; record when and how consent was given; tell the candidate how to withdraw. Set a concrete period (for example 12 months) and delete when it ends.

### Dansk (tekst til mail eller afslag)

> Vi vil gerne gemme din ansøgning i op til [12] måneder fra i dag, så vi kan kontakte dig, hvis vi får en stilling, der passer til din profil. Det gør vi kun, hvis du giver samtykke. Svar "Ja, I må gemme min ansøgning" på denne mail, hvis du er indforstået. Hvis du ikke svarer, sletter vi ansøgningen efter [6] måneder som normalt. Du kan altid trække dit samtykke tilbage ved at skrive til [e-mail til persondata], og så sletter vi ansøgningen.

### English (text for email or rejection)

> We would like to keep your application for up to [12] months from today so that we can contact you if a role matching your profile opens up. We will only do this with your consent. Reply "Yes, you may keep my application" to this email if you agree. If you do not reply, we will delete your application after [6] months as usual. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to [data protection email], and we will then delete your application.

### Short line for the privacy notice to applicants

- DA: "Ansøgninger fra kandidater, der ikke ansættes, slettes senest [6] måneder efter, at stillingen er besat, medmindre du har givet samtykke til, at vi gemmer den længere."
- EN: "Applications from candidates who are not hired are deleted no later than [6] months after the role is filled, unless you have consented to longer retention."
